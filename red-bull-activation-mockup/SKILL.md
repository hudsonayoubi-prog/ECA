# Red Bull activation mock-up

## Principles
1. Supplied references are the source of truth. Never redesign, replace or invent a Red Bull tool.
2. Build progressively. Add each new item to the same master concept and keep everything already approved unless told to change it.
3. Keep scale. Use the dimensions given; mark anything estimated as a placeholder (dashed in plan, labelled in 3D).
4. Flag missing information instead of assuming something that changes the design. Never invent logo artwork; use a labelled stand-in.
5. Be honest about the output. A block model or plan is not a photoreal render; say so and offer a scene brief for an image or 3D tool.

## Per new item (reply in this order, briefly)
- Where to position it.
- Why that position works operationally and visually (circulation, queuing, sightlines, staff access, shade, ground contact).
- What dimensions or information are still needed.
Then update the plan, the 3D model and the item register together.

## Deliverables
- Plan: top-down, to scale, solid for supplied sizes, dashed for placeholders.
- 3D block model: rotate and tilt controls, labels readable from the guest side.
- Specs board: locked specifications and a schematic elevation.
- Item register: item, quantity, sizes, placement, status.
- Handover doc: overview, base structure, items, assumptions, open questions, safety checks, next steps.

## Base structure (from the Snap Modular drawings and the Adelaide Structural certificate, 3 Feb 2026)
- Blaze Bar under the viewing deck; the serving front faces guests.
- Deck 9752 x 6058 mm, floor 3195 mm above ground, Ekodeck Moonlight P4 decking.
- Balustrade 1100 mm, open handrails, no signage on handrails.
- Stair opening 2917 mm wide; stairs are outside the engineer's scope and must be self-supporting.
- Four rear columns (SHS 89 x 6, 2945 mm) and a 20 kN tie-down at the container rear each side.
- Limits: 35 people, 4 kPa and 2.7 kN including furniture, strap down at 90 km/h, shelter at 110 km/h, ground 100 kPa minimum bearing.
- The certificate is not Building Rules Consent.

## Items supplied so far
- Parasol x 8: 2000 x 2000 x 2413 mm, steel base 500 x 500 mm. Can table x 8: diameter 670, 850 deep, 1080 high, 24 kg. Pole passes through the table.
- Can You Make It zone (6000 x 2000 mm): media moment wall 2000 x 400 x 2000; comms cube tower 350 x 350 x 1050 (three cubes, logo all sides); check-in desk 1500 x 400 x 1100; ice barrel diameter 406 x 915, 7.3 kg; digital poster stand 760 x 600 x 2250 with a 627 x 1097 portrait screen at 952 to 2049 mm. Run is 5578 mm left to right with 422 mm spare.

## Checks to raise every time
- Deck load against 2.7 kN, including all furniture and the parasol bases.
- Wind and parasols on an elevated deck.
- Power and cable routes for screens and lit items.
- Tie-down blocks clashing with items at the container rear.
- Canopy spacing and circulation to the stair landing.

## Open items at last handover
- Meaning of "dipos" (two either side of the desk) versus the single ice barrel.
- Logo and pistachio artwork; bar serving length and trough end; stair run; parasol weight; car park boundary, entries, north point and surface.
